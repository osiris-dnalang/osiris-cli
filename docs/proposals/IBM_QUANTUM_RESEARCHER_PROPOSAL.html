1. Executive Summary

As superconducting quantum processor arrays scale beyond 100 physical qubits, coherent spectator error—specifically static and driven inter-qubit ZZ crosstalk—emerges as a primary bottleneck limiting multi-qubit entanglement fidelity, complex scrambling depth, and quantum error correction thresholds.

2.1 Closed-Loop Bipartite Refocussing on Heavy-Hex

On the heavy-hex lattice, qubits are partitioned into bipartite sublattices $A$ and $B$. Synchronized pulses preserve ZZ Hamiltonian signs ($U_{\text{sync}} = e^{-i \frac{\zeta}{4} Z_A Z_B τ}$). Introducing a deliberate stagger $Δt$ inverts the interaction sign during the stagger window:

$$U_{\text{stag}}(τ) = e^{-i \frac{\zeta}{4} Z_A Z_B τ_1} (X_A \otimes I_B) e^{-i \frac{\zeta}{4} (-Z_A Z_B) Δt} (I_A \otimes X_B) e^{-i \frac{\zeta}{4} Z_A Z_B τ_2} \approx 𝕀$$
